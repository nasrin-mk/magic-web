How does booking work?

Send a short message with your date, venue and type of event. You will receive a reply with ideas and a quote, and the date is secured once the booking is confirmed.

Where does Magicnas perform?

Across Dubai and the wider Gulf region, at hotels, private homes, exhibition halls and corporate venues.

Can we see a performance first?

Yes. Watch the event reel on this page, and ask if you would like to see footage from a similar event to yours.

What about show length and technical needs?

Roaming sets and stage shows can be shaped to fit your schedule. Technical needs are minimal, and any requirements are confirmed with you well before the event.
