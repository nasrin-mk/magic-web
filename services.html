For Event Planners

Logistics & Practical Details

Seamless execution is just as important as the magic on stage. Here are the common questions event chairs and production directors ask before confirming dates.

What languages does Nas perform in?

Nas performs fluently in English, Malayalam, and Hindi, with polite Arabic stage interactivity. His visual and mentalism routines are designed specifically to eliminate language barriers across multicultural delegations.

Can multiple formats be combined for one event?

Yes. Many gala chairs book VIP Strolling Sleights during the 60-minute cocktail mixer, followed by the headline Mentalism Keynote on the main stage after dinner.

How are international flights and travel handled?

Nas is based in Dubai, UAE, and Kerala, India. For international bookings, travel logistics (business/economy flights, local ground transfers, and hotel accommodation) are simply appended to the performance contract.

Are the routines clean and corporate-appropriate?

100%. Nas has entertained sovereign families, diplomatic delegations, and multinational boards. The humor is witty, respectful, and never relies on crude or embarrassing volunteer interactions.
